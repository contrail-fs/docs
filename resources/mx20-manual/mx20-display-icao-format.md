## **Display ICAO for US Airports**

Press the Menu Item key to toggle on/off the display of ICAOs for US airports. When enabled, the leading "K" character for United States airports is displayed according to the ICAO format. When disabled, the leading "K" is not displayed.

#### **Altitude Units**

Press the Menu Item key to set the display of altitude in either feet or meters.
