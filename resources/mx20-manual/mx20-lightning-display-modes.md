## Lightning

The WX500 detects electrical discharges within a range determined by the installation of your Stormscope. These discharge locations are noted as Lightning strikes and displayed as a red "x." You may select either single Strike or Cell (groups) of lightning representations.

#### <span id="page-96-0"></span>**Strike**

Individual strikes are noted.

#### **Cell**

Only lightning strikes associated with a group, or cell, of strikes are displayed.
