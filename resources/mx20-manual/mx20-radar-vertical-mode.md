## **Vertical Mode (GWX 68 and ART2000/2100 only)**

The Vertical Mode (VERT) commands the radar head to scan in the vertical plane at a pilot selectable bearing off of the nose of the aircraft. In this mode, the current Bearing angle is shown on the lower half of the display in green.
