## **Graphical WX Overlay Options**

A variety of graphical information is available with the Overlay function that is available when viewing the Graphical WX display. Press the OVRL function key to view and select available options. Some or all of the following may be available, based on your FIS subscription.

- METARs, US, and/or Canadian (determined by subscription)
- SIGMETs & AIRMETs
- Echo Tops
- TFRs
- US and/or Canadian Weather Radar (determined by subscription)
- Label
- Flight Plan
- Legend
- Map Detail
- Map Orientation
