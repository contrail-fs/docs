## <span id="page-92-0"></span>**Sorting TAFs and METARs**

Displayed text products can be sorted by distance from your present position (Nearest Pos), distance from destination (Nearest Dest), or alphabetically (Alpha).

- 1. Press **Menu/Enter**.
- 2. Press the top menu item to toggle between the choices.
