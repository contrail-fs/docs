## **ILS/Localizer Depiction**

When either an ILS or localizer is tuned on the SL30, the MX20 will display the currently tuned approach on the appropriate airport runway. The SL30 Nav function provides an overall graphic view of the selected airport and approach based on runway extensions and the selected frequencies. The standard ILS symbol is shown in magenta on the MX20 display.
