## **Typical Approach Scenario**

Setting the destination waypoint to the destination airport on the GPS navigation system and pressing LOAD CHART from the Custom/IFR Map will present the destination airport in the selection list. Selecting this airport will then bring up the approach charts that are available for that airport, which can then be loaded by pressing the LOAD "smart" key.
