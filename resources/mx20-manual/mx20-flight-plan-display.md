### TRK Up Arc/TRK Up 360

This ring is marked in nautical miles from your airplane with the ring placed at one half the distance of the map scale. For instance if you have the map scales at 50 miles, the ring will be at 25 miles. The ring will change automatically as you change the map scale. The Terrain function display is always in the Track Up mode.
