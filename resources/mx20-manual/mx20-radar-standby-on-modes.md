## **Standby/On Mode**

In the standby mode, the radar head is powered up, but is not transmitting. Placing the unit into standby mode initiates the warm-up sequence that is required by the transmitter within the radar head assembly. Pressing the STBY/ON key while in Standby mode turns on the radar in the Weather Mode. While in the ON mode in both Map and Weather modes, the green RDR annunciator is on solid indicating that the radar is energized and transmitting.

#### **Test Mode**

Pressing the TEST key while in the Standby mode places the radar in the Test mode. The radar then sends a test pattern that is displayed on the MX20 I/O.
