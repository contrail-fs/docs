## **ADS-B Traffic (TRAF) Function**

<span id="page-52-0"></span>The Traffic Function allows you to view other traffic in the area, when installed with a UAT data link radio. The term "UAT" refers to the "GDL 90" UAT. This screen can also show your flight plan. Traffic is shown in relationship to your aircraft. "Smart" keys allow you to zoom in and out, show traffic labels, and select an individual traffic target. The Traffic function is not available in all software versions. Review the Limitations section in the front of this guide for the limitations that apply traffic data.
