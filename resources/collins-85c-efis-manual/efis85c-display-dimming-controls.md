# DIM Knobs

Rotate the DIM knobs to change the intensity of the EADI and/or EHSI.

The DIM control consists of a pair of concentric knobs to control each CRT's intensity. One knob controls the EADI; the other controls the EHSI. The knobs are sized such that they normally turn together, but may be turned independently to balance the displays. (See CAUTION on page 7 relating to operating the displays at maximum brightness.)
