# HLD (Hold) Button

Push the HLD button to "freeze" the display for detailed analysis of targets.

The HLD button is a push on/push off momentary button. Each push of the button toggles the display from normal operation to a display hold mode.

The display hold mode stays on until the HLD button is pushed to turn it off, or until the operating mode, display range, gain setting, GCS on or off, SLV on or off, or SEC on or off is changed, or until the mode switch is turned OFF. Targets shown on the EFIS displays are not updated in the display hold mode. The display hold mode is annunciated on EFIS as "HOLD" alternating with the selected mode (i.e., HOLD-WX-HOLD-WX etc.).
