# **Category II Outputs**

The EFIS-86C provides two Category II outputs that can aid the pilot in determining if Category II approach monitoring requirements are met. These outputs are the 'green' output and the 'amber' output, with each output driving appropriately colored annunciators.

#### NOTE

The pilot's and copilot's CAT II select switches, switching relays, and annunciator lights (CAT II green and amber light on both sides) are installer supplied items and are not furnished as part of the EFIS-86C system.

The CAT II 'amber' annunciator flashes for 2 seconds following a CAT II request to verify that the annunciator is working. Then, if all of the conditions listed below are met, the CAT II 'green' annunciator turns on. If all of the conditions listed below are not met, the CAT II 'amber' annunciator either turns on steady or flashes on and off. The on steady or flashing state of the 'amber' annunciator is determined by whether the 'amber' annunciation is caused by a condition 1 function, or by a condition 2 function (both of these conditions are listed in following paragraphs).
