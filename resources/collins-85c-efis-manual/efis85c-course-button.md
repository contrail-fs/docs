# CRS (Course) Button

Sequentially push the CRS button to step through the selections of navigation sensors to be connected to the EHSI active selected course arrow and lateral deviation bar (solid double bar course arrow).

The possible selections depend upon interconnect wiring (both strapping and installer supplied switching). The possible selections are VOR1/LOC1, LRN1 (which may be an INS, VLF, LRN, LNV, GPS or FMS), MLS1, "ADF1, VOR2, LRN2, MLS2 and "ADF2 ("-not available in TCAS versions). Holding the CRS button for longer than 1/2 second causes the course deletion to cycle directly to the primary VOR/LOC system (VOR1/LOC1 for the pilot, and VOR2/LOC2 for the copilot).

The active selected course navigation sensor is annunciated in the lower left center of the EHSI.

Active selected course data is stored if power is lost or if the system is turned off.
