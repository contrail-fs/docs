# **Navigation Sensors**

VOR, **ILS, MLS, TCN, ADF, and** LNAV-For navigation sensors in self-test mode the EADI and EHSI show the sensors fail flags non-flashing during the self-test. Vertical and lateral deviation displays are not removed from view during the test. When the sensors self-test is successfully completed, the fail flags turn off.
