# <span id="page-19-0"></span>DH/RNG (Decision Height/ Range) Knob

The dual function DH/RNG knob sets the decision height shown on the EADI and selects the range on the EHSI when in the sector formats and when weather radar (RDR button) is not selected.

PUSH DH Position — When pushed in, the decision height altitude appears on the EADI, and rotating the knob changes the DH setting in ±10 foot steps (clockwise rotation increases DH with maximum DH being 999 feet). A rapid turn of the knob gives a minimum 300 foot change in the DH. The DH display is removed when the DH/RNG knob is pulled out. It remains out of view until the knob is pushed in again, or until the aircraft descends through 1000 feet agl (radio altitude) at which time the DH display appears flashing for 10 seconds and is then removed from view.

PULL RANGE Position — When pulled out, rotating the knob selects range on the EHSI when either of the sector formats is being displayed and when RDR is not selected. A dashed cyan range arc is shown at midrange and one-half of the full scale range is displayed at the right end of the range arc. Available full scale ranges are 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 300 and 600 nml.
