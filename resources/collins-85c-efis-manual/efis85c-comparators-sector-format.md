# COMPARATORS, SECTOR FORMAT

#### **EHSI Category II Excessive Deviation Warnings**

In the EFIS-86C system, there are no special visual cues that appear on the EHSI to indicate CAT II excessive deviation warnings. All CAT II excessive deviation warnings appear on the EADI. Refer to the 'EADI Comparator Warnings' paragraphs for operational details on EADI comparators and EADI CAT II excessive deviation warnings.
