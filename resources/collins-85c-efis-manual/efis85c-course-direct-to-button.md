# PUSH CRS DIRECT (Course Direct To) Button

Push and release the PUSH CRS DIRECT button to automatically center the deviation bar with the course arrow pointing directly to the station.

On the EHSI display associated with the PUSH CRS DIRECT button, the selected active or second course arrow (selected by the appropriate DCP button) rotates to point to the station and the deviation bar centers.
