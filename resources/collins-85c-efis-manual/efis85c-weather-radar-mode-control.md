# **MODE** Control

Turn the MODE knob to select the operating mode of the TWR-850 radar.

OFF-Turns off the TWR-850 system.

STBY—Turns on the TWR-850 system but does not allow it to transmit.

TEST-Turns on the radar self-test display.

TGT—Turns on the radar receiver-transmitter to detect precipitation, but does not show any detected weather on the displays.

**MAP**—Turns on receiver-transmitter in the ground mapping mode.

**WX**—Turns on the receiver-transmitter in the basic weather detection mode. Detected precipitation may show as green, yellow, red, or magenta targets.

WX+T—Turns on the receiver-transmitter in the basic weather detection mode and also turns on the precipitation-related turbulence detection mode. Precipitation-related turbulence is detectable on only the 5, 10, 25, and 50 mile ranges.

TURB—Turns off all targets except detected precipitation-related turbulence.

#### RANGE Control

Turn the RANGE knob to set the desired maximum display range.

Turbulence is detectable in only the 5, 10, 25, or 50 mile ranges. Display range is controlled by the WXP any time radar information shows on the display. The selected range is annunciated on the EFIS displays at the half-range and full-range scale marks. (Full-scale range is not annunciated on some EFIS displays).

#### **GAIN Control**

Turn the GAIN knob to increase or decrease the gain from the CAL (calibrated) setting.

Normal system operation is with the knob in the CAL position. Settings other than CAL are annunciated on EFIS (i.e. G+2, G-3, etc.).
