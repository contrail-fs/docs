---
title: "Quickstart checklist"
---

This page lists the **minimum normal procedures** needed to get the Falcon 50 from a cold-and-dark cockpit through departure and the early climb, then through a typical arrival and shutdown. Walkaround items, detailed interior preflights, and **test** steps (fire warnings, GPWS, stalls, anti-skid exercises, and similar) are **omitted on purpose**—the simulator’s default cold-and-dark state is assumed to be airworthy, and those checks belong in a full normal checklist when you want every line item.

<Note>
**Caution (engine start):** Do not start No. 1 engine first; start **No. 2**, then **No. 3**, then **No. 1**, per normal procedures.
</Note>
