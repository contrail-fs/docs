---
title: "Ground Power"
---

The Falcon 50 electrical system can be powered by an external **Ground Power Unit (GPU)** when connected to the aircraft. Use of an external GPU is **atypical** for the Falcon 50 — the APU is normally used to provide electrical power on the ground, with the GPU reserved for situations where the APU is unavailable or maintenance procedures explicitly require it.

## Connection and Operation

A 28-volt DC ground power unit is connected to the receptacle behind the left wing. Once physically connected, ground power is enabled from the cockpit using the **power selector switch on the overhead panel**.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Voltage** | 28 volts DC |
| **Connection point** | Behind the left wing |

When external power is enabled, it is supplied directly to the **Left Hand bus**. To extend coverage to the **Right Hand bus** and its downstream Primary B and Auxiliary D busses, close the main bus tie switch on the overhead panel.

<Tip>
If you need full electrical coverage on the ground from a single GPU, close the main bus tie to extend external power to the Right Hand bus.
</Tip>

## Battery Isolation

The batteries are automatically isolated from the electrical system whenever external power is in use. This prevents the GPU from charging or interacting with the batteries through the main bus structure.

<Warning>
After disconnecting ground power, verify that **BAT1** and **BAT2** are connected if continued aircraft electrical operation is required. The batteries do not automatically rejoin the bus structure when ground power is removed.
</Warning>
