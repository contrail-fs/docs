---
title: "Low-Pressure Bleed Air"
---

Low-pressure (LP) bleed air is the **primary pneumatic supply** in normal operation. It is drawn from the LP compressor ports of all three engines and feeds a common manifold that supplies air conditioning, pressurization, and the secondary pressurization systems. LP air is available whenever the engines are running — there are no shutoff valves, so the only way to stop LP airflow from an engine is to shut it down.

## Main LP Bleed Air

The main LP bleed air is taken from one port on each engine (the LH and center engine right ports, and the RH engine left port) plus the optional APU port. These sources feed a **common distribution manifold** that supplies:

- Cabin, baggage compartment, and nose cone conditioning and pressurization
- Wing and center engine S-duct anti-icing systems

| Condition | Absolute Pressure | Temperature |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Ground, idle, no flow** | 1.12 bar (16.2 psi) | 38 °C (100 °F) |
| **Ground, take-off, no flow** | 6.5 bar (94 psi) | 270 °C (520 °F) |
| **In flight, 41,000 ft, M 0.75** | 1.73 bar (25 psi) | 200 °C (390 °F) |

Maximum air flow on the ground is **33 kg/min**.

## Secondary (Auxiliary) LP Bleed Air

A separate **secondary LP system** is supplied from the LH ports of the No. 1 and No. 2 engines only, independent of the main LP system. It has the same temperatures and pressures as the main system but is dedicated to pressurizing the aircraft's fluid reservoirs:

- Pressurization of the two **hydraulic reservoirs**
- Pressurization of the **fuel tanks** (wing tanks and feeder tanks)

Pressure regulating valves in this system step the air down to the correct level for each consumer. Drains from the LP bleed line and the fuel pressurization reducing valve are routed to an automatic fuselage drain. The No. 3 engine is not connected to any secondary system, and the right engine RH port is capped off.

<Note>
The secondary LP system has no pilot controls and no shutoff valves. It is supplied whenever Engines 1 and 2 are running and stops when they are shut down.
</Note>
