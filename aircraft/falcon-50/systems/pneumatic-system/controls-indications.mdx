---
title: "Controls & Indications"
---

The pneumatic system is controlled from the **overhead panel**, with warning lights on the master warning panel and the bleed pushbutton on the APU control panel. The overhead panel carries a blue schematic of the bleed-air system with the valve switches arranged around it.

## Overhead Panel Switches

Across the top of the schematic are four two-position toggle switches controlling the HP valves and the PRV. Across the bottom are three switches controlling conditioned airflow to each compartment.

| Switch | Positions | Function |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **HP 1 / HP 2 / HP 3** | AUTO / OFF | HP bleed valves; in AUTO they open automatically with anti-icing (OFF locked by a stud) |
| **PRV** | AUTO / OFF | Pressure regulating valve for air-conditioning make-up at low rpm |
| **CABIN** | OFF / ON / AUTO | Conditioned air to the passenger cabin |
| **CREW** | OFF / ON / AUTO | Conditioned air to the cockpit (nose cone) |
| **BAG** | OFF / ON | Opens a valve to pressurize the baggage compartment with cabin air |

In **AUTO**, the CABIN and CREW valves supply air on the ground with thrust levers below 54 % N1, and supply air in flight regardless of thrust lever position. In **ON**, the valves are open under all conditions; in **OFF**, airflow is shut off.

## Isolation Valve

In the center of the schematic is a rotary **ISOLATION** switch that controls a motor-operated isolation valve in the common manifold. The valve is normally **open**, so air from any engine or the APU feeds the whole system. Rotating the knob 90° to ISOLATION closes the valve and splits the manifold into independent left and right sub-systems.

Closing the isolation valve is used for three situations:

- A leak in either sub-system (limits the leak to one side; one conditioning system remains, sufficient for pressurization and air conditioning)
- Unwanted airframe or No. 2 engine anti-ice operation
- Smoke in the air-conditioning system

<Tip>
In the OPEN position the blue bar on the ISOLATION knob is aligned with the system diagram painted on the panel.
</Tip>

## Warning Lights

Two amber lights on the master warning panel monitor the pneumatic system.

| Light | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **BLEED OVHT** | Overheat in a bleed-air system or at a conditioning inlet (thermostats set to 300 °C / 572 °F) |
| **APU BLEED** | APU bleed valve open when it should be closed |

The BLEED OVHT detection uses five thermostats — one per HP bleed system and one per conditioning system. If BLEED OVHT illuminates while the anti-icing systems are off, the PRV is the most likely cause.

<Warning>
To identify a faulty bleed system, set the **HP 1, HP 2, HP 3, and PRV** switches to OFF one at a time. If the light keeps steady, return that switch to AUTO and try the next. When the light **flashes**, the last switch actuated identifies the faulty system — leave it OFF and follow the checklist. The light extinguishes once the system cools to 265 °C (510 °F).
</Warning>
