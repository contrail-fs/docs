---
title: "APU Bleed Air"
---

The Auxiliary Power Unit can supply LP bleed air to the common manifold and to the secondary HP manifold while the aircraft is **on the ground**. This provides enough air to run the air-conditioning system before the engines are producing sufficient bleed air, and feeds the jet pump used for cabin pressurization control.

| Parameter | APU Port (on ground) |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Absolute pressure** | 3.3 bar (48 psi) |
| **Temperature** | 240 °C (464 °F) |
| **Air flow** | 20 kg/min (44 lb/min) |

## Operation

APU bleed air is available only when the APU speed is at or above **95 %** and all engine thrust levers are below **54 % N1** (54° PLA). Bleed air is extracted through a butterfly valve controlled by the **BLEED** pushbutton on the APU control panel (right console), which illuminates green when bleed is commanded. Pressing the button again closes the valve and extinguishes the light.

The valve closes **automatically** whenever at least one throttle is advanced above 54 % N1. This prevents engine bleed air from being re-ingested by the APU through its bleed port.

<Warning>
APU bleed air should **never** be used while the anti-icing systems are in use.
</Warning>

## APU BLEED Warning Light

An amber **APU BLEED** light on the warning panel indicates that the APU bleed valve is open when it should be closed.

- With the APU shut down or running below 95 %, the light indicates that the bleed valve is **not fully closed**.
- With the APU running above 95 % but a throttle above 54 % N1, it indicates that the **automatic closing function is inoperative**.

<Note>
There is a four-second time delay between the command to close the valve and illumination of the APU BLEED light.
</Note>
