---
title: "Reservoirs"
---

The hydraulic system uses **two identical reservoirs**, one per system, located in the aft compartment. Each reservoir supplies fluid to its pumps and receives the return flow after the fluid has been used. Both reservoirs are pressurized to prevent pump cavitation at altitude.

## Pressurization

Each reservoir is pressurized to **1.47 bar (21.3 psi)** by low-pressure bleed air taken from the Engine 1 and Engine 2 LP bleed ports, through a pressure-reducing valve. Air enters each reservoir through a pressurization valve assembly that includes an inlet valve, relief valves, and a monitoring pressure switch.

| Component | Setting | Function |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Pressure relief valve** | 1.8 bar (26 psi) | Limits maximum reservoir pressure |
| **Negative pressure relief valve** | 27 mbar (0.4 psi) | Prevents negative pressure |
| **Pressure switch** | 1.1 bar (16 psi) decreasing | Illuminates TK.P1 or TK.P2 amber light |

Pressurization prevents cavitation when the pumps draw fluid. If the **TK.P1** or **TK.P2** light illuminates, the corresponding reservoir is depressurized.

<Warning>
Should either **TK.P1** or **TK.P2** illuminate, monitor the fluid quantity and pressure indicators. If pressure begins to fluctuate, descend below **20,000 ft** as soon as conditions permit.
</Warning>

## Capacity and Quantity

The maximum usable capacity of each reservoir is approximately **12 liters**, with a volume up to the filling level of **9.85 liters**. Fluid level can be checked on the ground through a window on the side of each reservoir, allowing the reservoir to be topped up before flight if necessary.

The gauging system uses a float valve and a potentiometer transmitter, with fluid level shown on the **left scale** of each double pressure/level indicator on the hydraulic panel. There is a red range between **MINI and 1/4** and a green range from **5/8 to MAX**.

<Note>
Indicated fluid level changes during normal operation. In **System 1**, the level drops about 13 mm when the system is pressurized (fluid stored in the accumulators) and rises about 41 mm during landing gear retraction, reversing on extension. In **System 2**, the level drops about 20 mm when the system is pressurized.
</Note>

## Filtering

Both the filling port and the suction canister of each reservoir are equipped with filters to keep contaminants out of the hydraulic fluid.
