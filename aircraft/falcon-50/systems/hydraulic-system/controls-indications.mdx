---
title: "Controls & Indications"
---

The hydraulic control and indication panel is located on the **lower center instrument panel**, on the pilot's side. It brings together the fluid level and pressure indicators for both systems, the stand-by pump control, the system warning lights, and the brake selector controls.

## Pressure and Quantity Indicators

The panel carries two double indicators, one per system, located in its center. Each indicator combines a fluid quantity scale and a pressure scale.

| Scale | Reads | Markings |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Left scale** | Reservoir fluid level | Red between MINI and 1/4; green from 5/8 to MAX |
| **Right scale** | System pressure | Green range from 2,800 to 3,000 psi |

## Warning Lights

The panel includes a set of amber lights that monitor the pumps and reservoirs. All of them can be checked with the warning panel **TEST** pushbutton.

| Light | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **PMP 1 / PMP 2 / PMP 3** | Associated engine-driven pump pressure below 1,500 psi |
| **TK.P1 / TK.P2** | Corresponding reservoir depressurized |
| **ST.PMP** | Stand-by pump run time exceeds 60 s, or selector in GROUND-TEST |

<Note>
All hydraulic panel lights are amber. Use the warning panel TEST pushbutton during the cockpit power-up flow to confirm that every light illuminates.
</Note>

## Stand-by Pump Control

The stand-by pump is operated by a three-position **STBY PUMP** lever on the right side of the panel:

- **AUTO** — the pump activates automatically when System 2 pressure falls below 1,500 psi (with conditions met)
- **ON** — the pump runs continuously
- **OFF** — the pump is inhibited

## Brake Controls and Indications

The panel also carries the braking system selector and its indications.

| Control / Light | Function |
|-----------------|----------|
| **L and R green lights** | Indicate braking on System 1 |
| **#2 P.BK yellow light** | Indicates braking on System 2 |
| **Braking system selector** | Three-position selector for the braking source |
| **ASKID pushbutton** | Antiskid test |

## Normal Operation

The hydraulic system requires only routine monitoring in normal operation:

- **Prior to engine start** — check that reservoir levels are in the green range and test the stand-by pump and lights.
- **While taxiing** — confirm levels remain correct after operating the flight controls, indicating no leaks.
- **Before approach and landing** — verify levels are correct for a normal approach and landing (minimum **1/2 scale**).
