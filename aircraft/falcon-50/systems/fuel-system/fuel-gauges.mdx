---
title: "Fuel Gauges"
---

Three fuel quantity gauges are located in the center instrument panel, one for each fuel subsystem (left, center, right). A single TOT–REAR selector switch controls all three gauges and has two positions.

## Gauge Selector Positions

| Gauge Position | Display | Description |
|----------------|---------|-------------|
| **REAR** | Feeder tank quantity only | Normal operating position. Shows only the feeder tank quantity. |
| **TOT** | Total subsystem fuel | Shows forward tank + feeder tank for the selected subsystem (left, center, or right). |

## Gauge Indications

In the **REAR** position, the gauges indicate only the fuel quantity in the feeder tanks.

In the **TOT** position, the gauges indicate the total fuel in the feeder tanks and the corresponding forward tanks (wing tanks or center wing tank plus associated feeder tank).

## Fuel Quantity Monitoring

During flight, the fuel quantity must be checked to ensure that fuel quantity in the tanks decreases as expected. This check is accomplished by positioning the selector to TOT from time to time.

When a forward tank is empty, **609 lb** will be indicated on the gauge in the TOT position. The 609 lb represents the amount remaining in the feeder tank.

<Note>
The fuel quantity indicators could show slightly higher values than the quantity actually loaded. The difference can be as much as 200 to 300 pounds with 3,000 pounds in one set of tanks (wing tanks, or center wing plus associated feeder tank).
</Note>
