---
title: "Engine Feed System"
---

The engine feed system supplies fuel from the feeder tanks to the engines under all normal and abnormal operating conditions.

## Feeder Tanks

Three feeder tanks are installed in the rear fuselage, one per engine.

| Feeder Tank | Maximum Capacity |
|------------|------------------|
| Left Feeder Tank | **≈ 1,400 lb** |
| Center Feeder Tank | **≈ 2,460 lb** |
| Right Feeder Tank | **≈ 1,400 lb** |

During flight, feeder tanks are automatically regulated to a nominal quantity of **609 lb**.

## Feeder Tank Regulation

The fuel system automatically maintains each feeder tank at **609 lb** during flight through a regulation system.

**Regulation logic:**

- **Above regulation level:** Transfer from forward tanks is inhibited until quantity decreases to 609 lb
- **Below regulation level:** Fuel is automatically transferred from the associated forward tanks
- **Insufficient fuel:** When remaining fuel is insufficient to maintain regulation, feeder tank quantity decreases normally

This regulation ensures a constant fuel head for engine supply.

<Note>
The 609 lb regulation level provides a buffer of fuel in each feeder tank, ensuring continuous engine feed even during transfer pump cycling or brief interruptions in fuel transfer.
</Note>

## Booster Pumps

Each feeder tank contains a **booster pump** that supplies positive-pressure fuel to the engine-driven fuel pump.

- Engines depend on their associated feeder tank booster pump for normal operation
- Low pressure downstream of a booster pump triggers the corresponding **FUEL** warning indication

<Warning>
Booster pump failure results in loss of positive pressure fuel supply to the engine. The FUEL warning will illuminate, and crossfeed may be required to maintain engine operation.
</Warning>

## Crossfeed Valves

**Crossfeed valves** allow one feeder tank to supply more than one engine.

- Normally closed during standard operations
- Used following a booster pump or feeder tank failure
- Permit continued engine operation using a single feeder tank if required

<Warning>
Crossfeed operations should only be used during abnormal conditions. Monitor fuel quantities closely when using crossfeed, as a single feeder tank will supply multiple engines.
</Warning>
