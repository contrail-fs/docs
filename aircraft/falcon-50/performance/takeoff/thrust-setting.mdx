---
title: "Thrust Setting"
---

No. 2 engine N1 settings for takeoff and maximum continuous thrust, by pressure altitude and temperature. Values apply to the **center engine**; reduce N1 by **0.4%** for the outboard (lateral) engines.

<Note>
  Use **OAT/SAT** when setting takeoff thrust. Use **TAT** when setting maximum continuous thrust.
</Note>
