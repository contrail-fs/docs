---
title: "Max Cruise Thrust (MCT)"
---

Maximum Cruise Thrust (MCT) performance data for the Falcon 50 at high-altitude flight levels. Each table shows the recommended cruise settings and resulting performance at MCT for a given pressure altitude and temperature deviation from ISA.

Columns represent aircraft weight in thousands of pounds. Row values are:

- **Ind. M.** — Indicated Mach number
- **NM/lb** — Nautical miles per pound of fuel (fuel efficiency)
- **TAT (°C)** — Total Air Temperature
- **TAS (kt)** — True Airspeed in knots
- **RPM (%)** — Engine N1 as a percentage
- **F/F (lb/hr)** — Total fuel flow in pounds per hour

<Note>
  Blank cells (—) indicate that MCT data is not available for that weight at the given altitude and temperature.
</Note>
