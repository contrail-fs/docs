---
title: "Icing Conditions"
---

Guidance for operating the Falcon 50 in icing conditions, including when anti-ice systems should be used, minimum engine speeds, and related approach and slat procedures.

## When icing conditions apply

Treat icing as a factor when **any** of the following is true:

- **In flight:** total air temperature (TAT) is **10°C or below** and visible moisture is present—clouds, fog with visibility of one mile or less, rain, snow, sleet, or ice crystals.
- **On the ground or for takeoff:** outside air temperature (OAT) is **10°C or below** and visible moisture is present.
- **On the ground or for takeoff:** OAT is **10°C or below** while operating on ramps, taxiways, or runways where snow, ice, standing water, or slush may be ingested by the engines or may freeze on the nacelles or engine sensor probes.

## Anti-ice system limits

### Engine anti-ice

Do not use engine anti-ice when total air temperature is **above +10°C**.

### Wing anti-ice

Do not use wing anti-ice when total air temperature is **above +10°C**.

Wing anti-ice must **not** be used on the ground except for brief checks carried out per the *Airplane Flight Manual* or *Maintenance Manual*.

<Warning>
  Avoid extended flight in icing conditions while configured with **Slats + 20° flaps** or **Slats + 48° flaps**.
</Warning>

## Minimum N1 speeds

In icing conditions, keep engine N1 at or above the values below for the current TAT range.

| Condition | –30 to –20°C | –20 to –10°C | –10 to 0°C | 0 to +10°C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cruise | 84% | 81% | 78% | 73% |
| Approach | 78% | 78% | 78% | 73% |
| One engine inoperative | 91% | 88% | 84% | 80% |

## Approach in icing

If N1 falls below the minimum during approach, deploy **airbrakes** as needed to hold the required N1 and fly higher approach speeds:

| Position | Speed |
| --- | --- |
| Position 1 | V<sub>REF</sub> + 10 kt |
| Position 2 | V<sub>REF</sub> + 15 kt |

<Note>
  Avoid extending airbrakes within **500 feet** of the ground.
</Note>

## Slats fail to retract in icing

If the slats do not fully retract after retraction is commanded in icing conditions (red transit light illuminated):

1. Limit airspeed to **V<sub>FE</sub> (200 KIAS)** or below.
2. Leave **wing anti-ice** on and maintain engine N1 at or above the minimum for the current conditions (see table above).

## Windshield anti-icing

<Warning>
  Use the **MAX** position on the windshield PILOT and COPILOT switches only when **NORM** heat is insufficient for the icing encountered **in flight**. Do not rely on MAX as the default setting.
</Warning>
