This page lists the **minimum normal procedures** needed to get the Falcon 50 from a cold-and-dark cockpit through departure and climb phases, then through a typical arrival and shutdown. Walkaround items, detailed preflights, and **test** steps (stalls, fire warnings, and similar) are **omitted on purpose**, as are checklist lines that are **already satisfied by the aircraft cold-and-dark state** if you load in that configuration.

## Power-up and APU

This section follows **PRESTART → (A) APU Start**. The full normal checklist also covers other ways to get electrical and bleed available before engine start—for example **No. 2 engine as APU**, **battery-only** starts, and **GPU (external power)**—which are not detailed here. Test steps (for example fire warning test) are skipped here.

## Engine start

<Note>
  Start **No. 2** first. With No. 2 running, **its generator helps power** the remaining starts on the DC system; starting an outboard first can cause **electrical eccentricities**. **No. 1** and **No. 3** may be started in **either order** after No. 2.
</Note>

### Ground Start

**Each engine, start selector on GRD START**

1. Press and hold that engine’s **start pushbutton** about **2–3 seconds** so the starter-generator engages (you can release once the start sequence maintains).
2. Confirm **N2** rises—if it does not reach **10%** within about **6 seconds**, abort per standard callouts.
3. When **N2 is above 10%**, advance the **power lever** from **STOP** to **IDLE** (fuel on; with **GRD START** selected, ignition is armed and cuts off automatically at **50% N2**).

The same sequence applies to **No. 1** and **No. 3** after No. 2. Continue to monitor **ITT**, **oil pressure**, and **N1** through idle per the full procedure.

## Transition altitude

When climbing through **transition altitude**, set the altimeters to **standard pressure (STD)** and finish the items below. In the **U.S.**, that step is commonly associated with **18,000 ft**. In **Europe** and many other regions, transition altitude is **published per airport or airspace** and is often **much lower** (for example around **5,000 ft** in numerous areas - use your chart, NOTAM, or briefing).

| Item | Location | Target |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |

| Oxygen | <Badge color="blue">Right Pedestal</Badge> | AS REQUIRED |

_HEFOE:_ **H**ydraulic panel, **E**lectrical panel & circuit breakers, **F**uel panel, **O**xygen / pressurization / environmental, **E**ngine parameters—confirm normal.
