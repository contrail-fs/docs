The figures below reflect the aircraft as modelled in Microsoft Flight Simulator. Only limitations that are relevant to flying the aircraft in the simulator are listed here.

## General

| Specification | Value (Imperial) | Value (Metric) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engines | 3 × Honeywell TFE731-3-1C turbofans | — |
| Maximum passengers | 9 | — |
| Maximum range | ~3,100 nm | ~5,741 km |
| Maximum fuel capacity | 15,513 lbs | 7,037 kg |
