> "When you have seen one Falcon, you have seen one Falcon"

This is a popular saying among Falcon pilots, meaning that no two Falcons are alike. These aircraft come in many different configurations. We have built one of the more common cockpit layouts: a retrofitted Falcon 50 with Collins EFIS 85C flight displays (EADI and EHSI) and a Garmin MX20 multifunction display.
