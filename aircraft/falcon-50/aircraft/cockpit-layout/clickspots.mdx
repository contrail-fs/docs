### Performance notepad

The performance notepad should be used to collect takeoff speeds and landing speeds for the FA50. Calculations are updated automatically based on the current aircraft weight and balance. After setting up the weight and balance in the EFB, click "Sync Weight" to update the calculations. Click "Sync Bugs" to update the speed bugs on the speed indicator.

<Frame>
  ![perf_notepad](/images/perf_notepad.jpg)

| --- | --- |
| **1** | Sync Bugs / Sync Weight |
| **2** | Change page |
| **3** | Stow performance notepad |
| **4** | Change between "Slats + 0° Flaps" and "Slats + 20° Flaps" |
| **5** | Change runway state: Dry or Wet |

### Sunshades

To use the sunshade, first select it from the side bins (one on the captain side, one on the first officer side). To manipulate it, drag the shade itself left and right to the desired position (1). To stow it back in the bin, click the center fixing of the sunshade (2).

<Frame>
  ![sunshade_click](/images/sunshade_click.jpg)
</Frame>

### Ground crew parking sign

The ground crew parking sign is placed on the captain side of the windshield upon loading into the aircraft. It is automatically flipped between "BRAKES OFF" and "BRAKES ON" by use of the park brake (1). To stow the sign into the bin, click the sign itself (2).

## Clickspot assists

### Cruise autothrottle

The Falcon 50 rarely came fitted with an autothrottle. However, we have simulated one as a quality-of-life feature should you wish to use it for long cruises. The autothrottle can be used for two stages of flight:

- **In climb** - the throttles will hold Max Climb Thrust
- **In cruise** - the throttles will hold your current indicated airspeed

The autothrottle will automatically disengage when starting the descent.

To activate the autothrottle, click the top left screw of the throttle assembly. Any manual adjustment of the throttles will automatically deactivate the autothrottle. Click the screw again after adjusting to re-activate the autothrottle.

<Frame>
  ![at_clickspot](/images/at_clickspot.jpg)
</Frame>

### EADI / EHSI

The autopilot controls are located in the center console. As a quality-of-life feature, autopilot shortcuts have been positioned around the EADI and EHSI.

<Note>
  Autopilot control labels can be added to the EADI and EHSI via a toggle switch in the EFB. In the FA50 app, go to **Options → Cockpit** and toggle **"AP Control Labels"**.
</Note>
