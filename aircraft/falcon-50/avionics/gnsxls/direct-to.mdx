---
title: "Direct To"
description: "Using the Direct To (D) key on the GNS-XLS to proceed direct to a flight plan waypoint or the nearest airport."
---

The Direct To section is accessed with the `D→` (Direct To) key. It lets you proceed direct to any waypoint — ahead of or behind the aircraft — or to the nearest airport.

## Direct To a waypoint

The first Direct To page lists all active flight plan waypoints across as many pages as needed. When the page is accessed, the cursor is positioned over the current **TO** waypoint. Position the cursor over any desired identifier to proceed direct to it. A new waypoint may also be added to the flight plan to proceed direct.

Waypoints may be annotated with the following:

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| **HP** | Holding Pattern |
| **PT** | Procedure Turn |
| **MAP** | Missed Approach Point |
| **FAF** | Final Approach Fix |
| **IAF** | Initial Approach Fix |

## Direct To the closest airport

The **DIR CLOSEST ARP** page is the last page in the Direct To section. Up to nine airports are displayed in order of their proximity to the aircraft, with the closest listed first.

<Note>
Airports listed on this page have runways 3,000 ft long or greater and are hard surfaced.
</Note>

<Note>
For a vertical Direct To that also sets up a path descent, see [VNAV Direct To](/aircraft/falcon-50/avionics/gnsxls/vnav#vnav-direct-to).
</Note>
