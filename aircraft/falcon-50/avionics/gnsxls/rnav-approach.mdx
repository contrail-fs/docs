---
title: "RNAV Approach"
description: "Flying an RNAV approach with lateral and vertical guidance on the GNS-XLS."
---

The GNS-XLS can provide lateral and vertical guidance for an RNAV approach.

<Warning>
The autopilot is not as precise as in modern aircraft. Monitor the approach closely and be prepared to intervene.
</Warning>

1. Select an RNAV approach on the **APPROACH** page.
2. Ensure the **NAV/GPS** switch is set to **GPS** for the entire approach.
3. When approaching the FAF, select **AUTO GS** mode.
4. The autopilot tracks the lateral and vertical path automatically.
