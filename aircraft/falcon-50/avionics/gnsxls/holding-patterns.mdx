---
title: "Holding Patterns"
description: "Setting up and canceling holding patterns on the GNS-XLS using the HOLD key."
---

A holding pattern is programmed with the `HOLD` key when the cursor is positioned over a waypoint identifier.

## Setting up a holding pattern

1. Press the `NAV` or `FPL` key to display the applicable page.
2. Press the line select key to position the cursor over the desired waypoint.

<Note>
On the navigation pages, only the **TO** waypoint can be selected. On the active flight plan page, a holding pattern cannot be programmed at the **FR** or **TO** waypoint.
</Note>

3. Press the `HOLD` key to display the **HOLDING PATTERN** page with the cursor over the **INBOUND CRS** field.
4. Verify or insert the **INBOUND CRS**.

<Note>
When an inserted inbound course value is beyond the direct entry parameters, a TEARDROP or PARALLEL pattern is programmed.
</Note>

5. Press the `ENTER` key. The entry type is displayed and the cursor moves to the **LEG TIME** field.
6. Verify or insert the **LEG TIME** (valid range 1.0 to 9.9 min).

### Optional: turn direction

7. Press the line select key to position the cursor over **TURN DIR**.
   1. Press the `BACK` key to change direction.
   2. Press the `ENTER` key.

### Optional: leg distance

8. Press the line select key to position the cursor over **LEG DIST**.
   1. Insert or verify the leg distance (valid range 1.0 to 50 nm).
   2. Press the `ENTER` key.

### Selecting the exit mode

9. Press the line select key to position the cursor over **MANUAL** or **AUTO**, then press the `BACK` key to change the exit mode. **MANUAL** initiates a continuous hold. **AUTO** exits the hold the next time the HP fix is crossed, or after the entry procedure is completed.
10. Press the `ENTER` key. An **OK? ENTER** prompt appears — verify your inputs.
11. Press the `ENTER` key to load the holding pattern at the selected waypoint.

## Canceling a holding pattern

1. On the **HOLDING PATTERN 1/1** page, press the line select key to position the cursor over the **CANCEL?** prompt.
2. Press the `ENTER` key. The HP annunciation is erased.
