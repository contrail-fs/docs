---
title: "Fuel Gauges"
---

Three fuel quantity gauges are located in the center section of the front cockpit, one for each fuel subsystem (left, center, right). A single selector switch controls all three gauges and has two positions.

## Gauge Selector Positions

| Gauge Position | Display | Description |
|----------------|---------|-------------|
| **REAR** | Feeder tank quantity only | Normal operating position. Shows only the feeder tank quantity. |
| **TOT** | Total subsystem fuel | Shows forward tank + feeder tank for the selected subsystem (left, center, or right). |

## Normal Operations

During normal flight operations, the **REAR** position is the standard setting. This position displays only the feeder tank quantity. The fuel system automatically maintains feeder tanks at **609 lb** during flight, so the REAR position typically indicates approximately 609 lb when forward tanks contain fuel.

## Empty Tank Indications

When a forward tank is empty, both gauge positions will indicate the same value. The REAR position continues to show **609 lb** (or current feeder tank quantity), and the TOT position shows the same value since the forward tank contribution is zero.
