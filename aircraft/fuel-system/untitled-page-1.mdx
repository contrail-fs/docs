---
title: "Forward Tanks"
---

The Falcon 50 forward fuel tanks provide **fuel storage only**. They do not supply fuel directly to the engines.

### Tank Arrangement and Capacities

| Tank            | Usable Fuel  |
| :-------------- | :----------- |
| Left Wing Tank  | **3,748 lb** |
| Center Tank     | **2,749 lb** |
| Right Wing Tank | **3,748 lb** |

Each wing tank is subdivided into **outboard** and **inboard** sections by internal structure.

### Wing Fuel Distribution

Fuel within each wing tank is managed to ensure that fuel from the **outboard (more aft) sections** is consumed first. This supports a more forward center of gravity throughout the flight.

This redistribution is achieved using **wing jet pumps** and passive gravity features, described in the [Fuel Transfer System](/aircraft/fuel-system/fuel-transfer-system) section.

### Tank Pressurization

All forward tanks are pressurized by **low-pressure bleed air from Engine 1 and Engine 2**.\
Pressurization occurs automatically when either engine is operating.

Forward tanks are maintained at a **higher pressure than the feeder tanks**, enabling fuel transfer toward the feeder tanks by **differential pressure**.
